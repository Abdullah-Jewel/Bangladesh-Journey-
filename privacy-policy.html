Privacy Policy

Bangladesh Visited Map – last updated: October 7, 2026

Bangladesh Visited Map (“the app”) is made by Abdullah Mukter Jewel. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

The app itself has no accounts and no sign-up, and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts. It does not ask for your precise location, camera, microphone, or contacts. The districts you mark are not sent to us.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and (if you consent, where required) personalise ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. Learn more: How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

The districts you mark as visited are saved only on your own device, inside the app’s local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. Uninstalling the app or tapping “Clear all” removes it.

Sharing

If you tap “Copy share link” (or share), the app creates a web link that contains your list of visited districts. Anyone you send that link to can see your map. Sharing is always your choice, and nothing is sent to us.

Third-party services

Besides Google AdMob (above), the app may load the “Hind Siliguri” font from Google Fonts to display Bengali text when an internet connection is available. These requests are handled by Google and are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

Map data

District boundaries are from geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0), simplified for this app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
